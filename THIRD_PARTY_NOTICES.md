# Third-party notices

The macOS package builds its helper with headers from Karabiner VirtualHIDDevice
v8.0.0. Karabiner's top-level license and the licenses in its vendor headers
apply to the helper build. The driver and daemon are installed separately.

- [Karabiner VirtualHIDDevice source and license](https://github.com/pqrs-org/Karabiner-DriverKit-VirtualHIDDevice/tree/v8.0.0)
- [BlackHole](https://github.com/ExistentialAudio/BlackHole) is installed separately.
- [python-sounddevice](https://github.com/spatialaudio/python-sounddevice)
  and [pySerial](https://github.com/pyserial/pyserial) are installed from the
  Python dependency lists.
